## 1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect data from two main sources: data you provide directly, and data collected automatically through your use of the Services.

### 1.1 Data You Provide Directly

When you submit a form, request support, or communicate with us, we may collect:
- Full name
- Business email address
- Phone number
- Job title or role
- Company name
- Region or country
- Product interest or inquiry category
- Free-text message content

If you include additional information in a message field (for example project details, deployment timelines, technical architecture, or procurement information), we may process that information as part of your request.

### 1.2 Data Collected Automatically

When you browse the Services, we may automatically collect:
- [IP address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_address)
- Device identifiers and device metadata
- Browser type, browser version, operating system, and language settings
- Referrer URL, visited pages, click paths, and interaction events
- Session timestamps and request logs
- Approximate location derived from network or device signals

In limited cases, technical events may include coarse location context (for example city-level telemetry inferred from IP geolocation providers). We do not guarantee geolocation precision and do not rely on it as a sole source of identity.

### 1.3 Operational Metadata

For reliability and security operations, we may collect:
- Error traces
- Failed authentication or anti-abuse events
- Performance diagnostics
- Infrastructure and API logs used to monitor service quality

You can jump to [Security](#8-security) for details on how these records are protected.

## 2. Sensitive Data and Third-Party Sourcing

We do **not** intentionally collect or process sensitive personal data, including special-category data such as health data, biometric identifiers, political opinions, religious beliefs, or similarly sensitive data categories.

Please do not submit sensitive data in message fields or uploads unless specifically requested through a lawful and documented process.

We also do **not** obtain your personal data from commercial data brokers or unrelated third-party enrichment providers as part of normal website operations. Our primary data source is your direct interaction with the Services and associated technical logs produced by those interactions.

If this approach changes in the future, we will update this Policy and clearly state what sources are used, what categories are collected, and why.

## 6. Cookies, Pixels, and Analytics

We and our partners may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies for:

- Preference persistence
- Reliability and debugging
- Audience measurement and analytics
- Advertising and campaign attribution

### 6.1 Categories of Tracking Technologies

| Category | Purpose | Typical Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | Core operation, request routing, security controls | Session or CSRF cookies |
| Functional | Preferences and UX continuity | Language or region settings |
| Analytics | Usage measurement and performance insights | Event and page-view metrics |
| Advertising / Attribution | Campaign measurement and audience relevance | Pixel-based attribution events |

### 6.2 Google Analytics

We may use [Google Analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Analytics) to understand traffic patterns, engagement, and feature usage trends. ### 6.3 How to Control Cookies

You can control cookie behavior by:
- Adjusting browser-level cookie settings
- Clearing stored cookies and local site data
- Blocking specific trackers using privacy tools

Disabling some tracking features may affect certain functionality, diagnostics, or personalization behavior.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including legal, operational, and security obligations.

### 7.1 General Rule

Our default retention principle is purpose limitation with time bounds. We do not retain personal data indefinitely by default.

### 7.2 Maximum Retention Commitment

Unless a longer period is required by law, legal hold, dispute resolution, or regulatory obligation, we do not retain personal data for any purpose longer than **one (1) year**.

### 7.3 Retention Summary

| Data Type | Primary Purpose | Standard Retention Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact form submissions | Reply to requests and business follow-up | Up to 12 months |
| Security and access logs | Threat detection, reliability, incident analysis | Typically 30-365 days |
| Consent and preference records | Compliance evidence and preference enforcement | Up to 12 months, unless law requires longer |

At the end of retention, data may be deleted, anonymized, or irreversibly aggregated, depending on legal and operational constraints.

## 8. Security

We implement technical and organizational safeguards designed to reduce risk, including access controls, network protections, logging, environment separation, and operational procedures.

Even with reasonable safeguards, no system can guarantee absolute security. Internet transmission and digital storage always involve residual risk, including unauthorized access, service abuse, credential compromise, or infrastructure attacks.

If we detect a material security event affecting personal data, we will respond according to applicable legal obligations and internal incident procedures.

For foundational context:

## 9. Children and Minors

Our Services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 18 for profiling or marketing purposes.

If we learn that a minor under 18 has provided personal data in violation of this Policy, we will take appropriate action, which may include account restriction, removal of relevant records, and related protective steps.

Parents, guardians, or authorized representatives may contact us to request review or deletion.
